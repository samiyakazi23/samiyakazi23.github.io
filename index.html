FloodWatch

Flood forecasts for Assam's rivers, tested on six monsoons

Predicts floods one to seven days ahead at 12 Brahmaputra-basin stations from river flow and rainfall, and runs every morning on its own. It was tested day by day on 2021 to 2026 and checked against the state disaster authority's district flood reports. Adding rainfall raised three-day early warnings in the 2022 floods from 11% to 42% of flood onsets, at a 0.9% false-alarm rate.

A U-Net maps floodwater from Sentinel-1 radar, which sees through monsoon cloud: 0.65 water IoU against 0.23 for the standard threshold method.

Python, LightGBM, PyTorch, Sentinel-1 SAR, GitHub Actions, D3.js

LitScout

A research agent that can't invent citations

Type a research question and it searches OpenAlex and arXiv, follows citation trails, reads the strongest papers and writes a review. Every cited sentence is checked against the paper it cites, and anything unsupported is cut. Tested against the reference lists of 7 published surveys, it found six times more of the experts' papers than keyword search.

Paper mode reads a GitHub repo and drafts a research paper about it, where every number has to match a line in the repo.

Python, LLM agents, Groq (gpt-oss, Qwen), OpenAlex and arXiv APIs, FastAPI, Docker

Zomato real-time lakehouse

A streaming data platform for a simulated food-delivery marketplace

Kafka streams orders, rider pings and reviews into Spark Structured Streaming and a Delta Lake lakehouse. Airflow and dbt build the analytics, and an 11-tab website shows them. A late-delivery model (ROC-AUC 0.71) scores every live order within seconds, and an AI assistant answers questions by writing read-only SQL.

Built with Smrithi Pillai. It scales from a million orders on a laptop to a billion-order backfill.

Kafka, Spark, Delta Lake, Airflow, dbt, PostgreSQL, Grafana, Spark MLlib, FastAPI
